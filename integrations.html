Hardware and Software Enforcement Specifications

Physical Diode Enforceability

Paladin AI operates strictly on the receiver (RX) termination of an optical fiber data diode. The transmitter (TX) laser is physically severed on the ingest interface, guaranteeing mathematical impossibility of packet injection or back-channel exfiltration into the monitored enclave.
