Operational Domains

Engineered For The Harshest Environments On And Off Earth

Autonomous systems operate where humans cannot go — in deep underwater trenches, high-radiation orbits, and contested airspace. BotBlox hardware guarantees continuous Ethernet connectivity under extreme physical stresses.

01 · Airborne Autonomy

UAVs & Aerospace Platforms

Modern unmanned aerial systems (UAS) carry high-definition optical payloads, LiDAR, FLIR cameras, and avionics autopilots. Legacy CAN and serial links cannot transport gigabit sensor data.

BotBlox delivers micro Gigabit Ethernet switches starting at just 8 grams, reducing harness weight by over 40% and providing uninterrupted communications across BlueUAS and NDAA-compliant defense programs.

Altitude: 30,000+ ft
Shock: 90g MIL-STD-810H
SWaP Optimized: 8g
02 · Orbital Regimes

Space & Satellite Systems

SmallSats and CubeSats deployed in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) require high-bandwidth interconnects between multi-spectral imagers, onboard processors, and downlinks.

BotBlox hardware has flight heritage with partners including TRL11, surviving launch vibration profiles, vacuum outgassing constraints, and radiation testing without destructive latch-up events.

LEO Heritage: TRL11 Flight Proven
TID Radiation Tested
Vacuum Outgassing Qualified
03 · Marine & Deep Ocean

Subsea ROVs & Marine Robotics

Inside underwater vehicles, electronic enclosures are space-restricted titanium pressure vessels with limited thermal dissipation.

Organizations like the Global Foundation for Ocean Exploration rely on BotBlox compact boards to connect 4K cameras, multibeam sonars, and telemetry at ocean floor depths exceeding 4,000 meters.
