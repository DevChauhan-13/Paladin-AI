Company Profile

We Build What No One Else Was Building

Founded in 2019, BotBlox Systems designs and manufactures miniature, rugged Ethernet switching, edge computing, and sensing hardware for platforms operating at the frontier of physics.

The Genesis

From Robotics Frustration to Industry Standard

In 2019, autonomous vehicle engineers were facing a painful paradox: robotic sensors, 4K cameras, and autopilots had shrunk to miniature sizes, but network routers were still bulky, heavy, heat-generating boxes designed for server racks or armored trucks.

BotBlox was founded by Josh Elijah in Brooklyn, New York to solve this exact bottleneck. We invented the micro-switch category: sub-30mm Gigabit Ethernet boards capable of withstanding extreme vibration, wide voltage fluctuations, and thermal extremes without requiring forced cooling.

Our 4 Core Engineering Axioms

01

Zero Wasted Volume

Every millimeter of PCB area and every gram of aluminum housing must justify its existence in platform flight endurance.

02

No Unregulated Surprises

Autonomous vehicle power rails are wildly noisy. Our boards must cleanly ingest 5V to 60V DC spikes without rebooting.

03

Line-Rate Testing

We never perform environmental shock and thermal tests on inactive hardware; all tests run at 100% network throughput.

04

Domestic Supply Security

All active components are vetted for continuous US availability, strict NDAA Section 889 rules, and rapid replenishment.
