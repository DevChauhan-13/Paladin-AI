WHAT IS A SOM?

A SOM is a pre-engineered compute brain you mount onto your own carrier board.

↳ SHOP SOMS
RUGGED SOM
A SYSTEM-ON-MODULE IS A TINY CIRCUIT BOARD THAT EMBEDS THE PROCESSOR, THE RAM, THE STORAGE, THE POWER MANAGEMENT, THE CLOCK GENERATION, AND A CURATED SET OF HIGH-SPEED PERIPHERALS ALL ROUTED, VALIDATED, AND CHARACTERISED ON A FOOTPRINT TYPICALLY SMALLER THAN A CREDIT CARD.
SOC DESIGN IS HARD. DDR MEMORY ROUTING REQUIRES IMPEDANCE-MATCHED, LENGTH-TUNED TRACES. MULTI-RAIL POWER SEQUENCING DEMANDS PRECISELY-TIMED REGULATOR RAMPS. EMI MITIGATION AROUND FAST CLOCKS NEEDS CAREFUL GROUND PLANES AND DECOUPLING. GET ANY OF THESE WRONG AND THE SYSTEM EITHER DOESN'T BOOT, FAILS CERTIFICATION, OR CRASHES IN THE FIELD AT 60°C. A SOM SHIPS WITH ALL OF THIS ALREADY SOLVED.

THE BOTBLOX SOM ADVANTAGE

A SOM for compute that stacks directly into our network hardware.

Our SOMs stack directly with BotBlox Ethernet switch hardware to add compute capability directly into the network. Instead of connecting a separate processor to a switch port, the SOM integrates with the switch itself, giving it direct access to Ethernet traffic while reducing system size, weight, and cabling.

INTEGRATION

Stacks directly onto our ethernet switch hardware

Rugged SOM and SAM9 Compute Module stack directly onto SwitchBlox Rugged and RouterCore (respectively), providing direct access to network traffic without occupying any Ethernet switch ports.

ENVIRONMENT

Survives where other SOMs fail

-40°C to +85°C operating, 30,000 ft altitude rated, 5-60 V input with reverse and overvoltage protection. Built for UAVs, ground robots, and field-deployed sensors. Rated just like our Ethernet hardware.

TINY FORM FACTORS

44.5mm x 44.5mm or smaller

Just like our ethernet hardware, our SOMs are designed to be as small as possible, optimized to fit in size and weight limited platforms.
